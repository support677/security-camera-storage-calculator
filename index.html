How this security camera storage calculator works

Storage per day = number of cameras × bitrate (Mbps) × 86,400 seconds ÷ 8 bits per byte × the share of the day the cameras record. A formatted drive gives you a little less than its label, so we count about 93% of the size as usable.

How many days will a 2TB hard drive record for 4 cameras?

With 4 cameras at 5MP, H.265, recording 24/7, a 2TB drive holds roughly 10–11 days. Switch to motion recording and it can stretch to several weeks.

Does H.265 really save storage?

Yes — H.265 needs roughly half the bitrate of H.264 for the same picture, so the same drive keeps about twice as many days.

Why does my recorder overwrite old footage?

DVRs and NVRs record in a loop: when the drive is full, the oldest footage is replaced. Size the drive for how far back you need to look — many businesses aim for 30 days.
